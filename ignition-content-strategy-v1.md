# Content Strategy — IGNITION Guide Launch
*Project Zero Point One | TikTok Content Strategy*
*Platform: TikTok | Cadence: 1 post per day | Duration: 30 days*

---

## Strategic Overview

This strategy has three goals working simultaneously — and they are not in conflict. They are a funnel.

```
Followers → Downloads → Email List
     ↑              ↑             ↑
  Visibility    Trust + CTA    Conversion
```

Content builds visibility and attracts followers. Followers encounter the IGNITION guide through consistent CTAs. Downloads capture email addresses. Every post serves at least one of these goals — the best posts serve all three.

---

## The Repurposing Model — One Idea, Three Formats

Every content idea is developed into three formats and posted across three consecutive days. This is the core content production architecture.

```
ONE IDEA
   ↓
Format 1 — Video (anchor points, talking head)         → Post Day 1
Format 2 — Carousel (slide by slide, saveable)         → Post Day 2
Format 3 — Caption (long-form text post or voiceover)  → Post Day 3
```

**The math:**
- 30 content ideas × 3 formats = 90 content pieces
- 90 pieces over 30 days = 3 posts per day
- 90 pieces over 45 days = 2 posts per day
- 90 pieces over 90 days = 1 post per day with full bank built ahead

**Choose your cadence based on capacity.** The bank is the same regardless. Building the content bank ahead of posting means you are never creating under pressure — which protects both quality and consistency.

**Why this model works:**
- The video reaches people who watch
- The carousel reaches people who read and save
- The caption reaches people who engage in comments
- One idea finds three different types of audience behaviour
- Each format reinforces the same core message — repetition builds positioning

**Format production order:**
Always write the video anchor points first. The carousel slides and caption are distilled versions of the same argument — they are faster to produce once the core thinking is clear.

## The One Person This Strategy Speaks To

**The Beginner.**

Someone who wants to start a digital marketing journey but does not know where to begin. Confused, curious, possibly overwhelmed. Watching content from people who are further along and feeling like they are already behind before they have even started.

Every piece of content is written and filmed for this one person. Not for experienced marketers. Not for people already in the game. For the beginner standing at the starting line wondering whether to run.

## Content Pillars

Three pillars. Every post belongs to one of them.

### Pillar 1 — THE JOURNEY 🗺️
*What is happening, what was learned, what the road looks like from inside it.*

This is the documentation content. Real, honest, unpolished enough to be believable. The audience follows because they are on the same road or about to be. This pillar builds **relatability and trust.**

Content types:
- Day-in-the-life of a digital marketing student
- Lesson recaps — what was studied and what it means in plain language
- Wins, no matter how small — normalising beginner-sized progress
- Honest reflections on what is hard, confusing, or unexpected
- Updates on Project Zero Point One — building the guide, setting up Beacons, etc.

---

### Pillar 2 — THE LESSONS 🎓
*What digital marketing actually is, taught simply, by someone who just learned it.*

This is the educational content — the Feynman method in action. Learn it, understand it, explain it simply. This pillar builds **authority and value.** It is also where the IGNITION guide's chapter content lives as bite-sized video versions.

Content types:
- "What I learned today" style teaching videos
- Myth-busting — common beginner misconceptions addressed directly
- Framework breakdowns — the Rose Garden Principle, SRT, GPS Framework, Know-Trust-Like
- Definitions made simple — what is a sales funnel, what is a lead magnet, what is SEO
- Beginner mistakes and how to avoid them

---

### Pillar 3 — THE MINDSET 🔥
*The thinking that makes the doing possible.*

This is the motivational content — but not generic motivation. Specific, grounded, experience-based. The kind of mindset content that makes a beginner feel seen and equipped rather than just temporarily fired up. This pillar builds **emotional connection and community.**

Content types:
- Skills before sales — why the classroom comes first
- The two masters problem — what divided attention costs you
- Redefining profit — why followers and email addresses are profit too
- Permission to be a beginner — you are allowed to be awkward, you are not allowed to not post
- African entrepreneurship and visibility — the audacity to be seen
- The Rose Garden Principle — brilliant work without visibility serves no one

## Content Rotation

One post per day. Rotate across the three pillars so the feed stays varied and no single type of content dominates.

**Suggested weekly rhythm:**

| Day | Pillar | Content Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | 🎓 Lessons | Start the week with value — teach something |
| Tuesday | 🗺️ Journey | Document what is happening this week |
| Wednesday | 🔥 Mindset | Mid-week motivation grounded in real experience |
| Thursday | 🎓 Lessons | Second teaching post of the week |
| Friday | 🗺️ Journey | End-of-week update or reflection |
| Saturday | 🔥 Mindset | Deeper, more personal — weekends allow longer attention |
| Sunday | 🗺️ Journey | Set-up for the week ahead — what is coming, what is being built |

This is a guide, not a rigid rule. If a strong idea arrives on a Monday that belongs to Mindset — post it. The rotation prevents stagnation. It does not override good instincts.

## The CTA Strategy

Every post has a CTA. The CTA rotates based on pillar and goal.

| Pillar | Primary CTA | Secondary CTA |
|---|---|---|
| 🗺️ Journey | Follow for more | Save this |
| 🎓 Lessons | Save this / Download the guide | Follow for more |
| 🔥 Mindset | Save this / Share this | Follow for more |

**The IGNITION guide CTA** is introduced naturally — not forced — in Lesson and Mindset posts where the guide's content is directly relevant. It should feel like a recommendation, not a hard sell.

Example CTA language:
- *"I broke this down in detail in my free beginner's guide — link in bio"*
- *"This is one of the lessons from IGNITION — download it free at the link in my bio"*
- *"If this resonated, the full chapter is in my guide — go grab it"*

**The email capture CTA** is embedded in the Beacons store flow — anyone who downloads the guide gives their email address as part of the process. The content does not need to explicitly ask for emails. It just needs to drive people to the link in bio.

## The Five-Format Model

Every content idea is developed into five formats. Three are face-shown or voice-led. Two are fully faceless. All five are produced in one batch session and scheduled in TikTok Studio.

```
ONE IDEA
   ↓
Format 1 — Talking Head Video          → Face shown    → Phone
Format 2 — Voiceover + B-roll          → Faceless      → Phone + TikTok editor
Format 3 — Carousel                    → Faceless      → Canva branded template
Format 4 — Caption (Text Post)         → Faceless      → TikTok native text post
Format 5 — Quote Card                  → Faceless      → Canva branded template
```

**The math:**
```
30 ideas × 5 formats = 150 content pieces
150 pieces ÷ 30 days  = 5 posts per day
150 pieces ÷ 45 days  = 3–4 posts per day
150 pieces ÷ 90 days  = ~2 posts per day
```

**Production rule:** Always develop the anchor points first. Every other format is a derivative — faster once the core argument is clear. One batch session of 2–3 hours produces 15–25 scheduled posts.

## Format Breakdown — Structure, Tools & CTAs

Each format has a primary algorithmic goal and a matching CTA designed to drive that specific behaviour.

---

**Format 1 — Talking Head Video**
*Tool: Phone | Goal: Watch time + Comments + Follows*

- Free-flow delivery for Journey and Mindset content — anchor points only, no script
- Structured teaching mode for Lessons content — key points noted, delivered clearly
- Hook in first 2–3 seconds — non-negotiable
- End on the CTA — never trail off

| Pillar | Best CTA |
|---|---|
| 🗺️ Journey | *"Follow along — I'm documenting everything as it happens"* |
| 🎓 Lessons | *"Follow for more beginner-friendly breakdowns"* |
| 🔥 Mindset | *"Save this for the next time you feel like quitting"* |

---

**Format 2 — Voiceover + B-roll**
*Tool: Phone + TikTok editor | Goal: Replays + Reach*

- Same anchor points as the talking head — delivered as voiceover
- Personal B-roll footage or text-on-screen as the visual layer
- Works well for reflective and lesson content where the visual does not need to be your face
- Slightly more polished feel — good for repurposing high-performing talking head ideas

| Pillar | Best CTA |
|---|---|
| 🗺️ Journey | *"Save this — you'll want to come back to it"* |
| 🎓 Lessons | *"Share this with someone starting their journey"* |
| 🔥 Mindset | *"Share this with a beginner who needs to hear it"* |

---

**Format 3 — Carousel**
*Tool: Canva branded template | Goal: Saves + Profile visits*

- 6–8 slides maximum
- Slide 1: Hook — bold, direct, stop-the-scroll
- Slides 2–6: One point per slide — short, readable, no waffle
- Slide 7: Practical takeaway or summary
- Slide 8: CTA slide — always the last slide
- Carousels are the highest-save format — every slide is a reason to save

| Pillar | Best CTA |
|---|---|
| 🗺️ Journey | *"Save this for when you hit this stage of your journey"* |
| 🎓 Lessons | *"Save this — you'll need it later"* |
| 🔥 Mindset | *"Save this for the hard days"* |

*Last slide CTA always includes:* Follow for more + link in bio for the guide where relevant.

---

**Format 4 — Caption (Native TikTok Text Post)**
*Tool: TikTok native text post | Goal: Comments + Engagement*

- Hook line first — same urgency as video
- Body: the full argument in short paragraphs — no walls of text
- Arrow points (→) for mobile readability
- Guide CTA embedded naturally in the body — not forced at the end
- Close with a question — this is what drives comments

| Pillar | Best CTA |
|---|---|
| 🗺️ Journey | *"Where are you at in your journey right now? Drop it below 👇"* |
| 🎓 Lessons | *"Which of these did you already know? Which surprised you? 👇"* |
| 🔥 Mindset | *"What's the thing holding you back right now? Be honest 👇"* |

*Always end with a question.* Comments are the metric this format is built for.

---

**Format 5 — Quote Card**
*Tool: Canva branded template | Goal: Shares + Saves + Brand recognition*

- One bold statement — a single line pulled from the idea's core argument
- Minimal text — the design carries it
- No explanation needed — the quote must land on its own
- Highest shareability potential of all five formats
- Build a library of these — they compound over time as brand assets

| Pillar | Best CTA |
|---|---|
| 🗺️ Journey | *"Save this reminder 🖤"* |
| 🎓 Lessons | *"Share this with someone who needs to hear it"* |
| 🔥 Mindset | *"Share this with a beginner who needs permission to start"* |

*Quote cards do not need a follow CTA — the brand consistency does that work passively.*

## Production Flow Per Batch Session

```
BATCH SESSION (2–3 hours, once or twice a week)
         ↓
Pick 3–5 ideas from the content bank
         ↓
For each idea:
  1. Write anchor points (core argument, hook, CTA)
  2. Film talking head video
  3. Record voiceover version (same anchor points)
  4. Build carousel in Canva template (swap text)
  5. Write caption (expand anchor points into paragraphs)
  6. Pull one quote for quote card (swap text in Canva)
         ↓
Schedule all formats in TikTok Studio
         ↓
Done. Let it run.
```

## Hook Strategy

The hook is the first 2–3 seconds. It determines whether the viewer stays or scrolls.

**Hook formulas that work for this content:**

For Journey posts:
- *"Day [X] of my digital marketing journey and here is what actually happened..."*
- *"Nobody told me this when I started digital marketing..."*

For Lessons posts:
- *"Here is what [concept] actually means in plain English..."*
- *"The biggest beginner mistake in digital marketing is..."*
- *"I just learned something that changed how I think about [topic]..."*

For Mindset posts:
- *"If you are waiting until you feel ready, watch this..."*
- *"The reason most beginners quit digital marketing is not what you think..."*
- *"This is what profitability actually looks like in your first month..."*

## Engagement Strategy

Visibility is not just algorithmic — it is relational.

**Daily engagement practice (15–20 minutes):**
- Leave 3–5 meaningful, specific comments on content from creators in the digital marketing / beginner journey niche
- Respond to every comment on your own posts within 24 hours
- Follow creators whose content you genuinely engage with

This is not optional maintenance. This is the mechanism that recalibrates the algorithm toward the right audience and builds the community that the content strategy is designed to attract.

## Metrics to Track

Three goals require three sets of metrics.

| Goal | Metrics to Track |
|---|---|
| **Grow followers** | Follower count, profile visits, follow rate from views |
| **Drive downloads** | Link-in-bio clicks, Beacons page visits, guide downloads |
| **Build email list** | Email addresses captured, welcome email open rate |

**Supporting metrics** (health indicators, not primary goals):
- Views per post
- Watch time / completion rate
- Saves (strongest signal for educational content)
- Shares (strongest trust signal)
- Comments (community building indicator)

**Review cadence:** Weekly — every Sunday, spend 10 minutes reviewing the week's metrics before planning the next week's content.
