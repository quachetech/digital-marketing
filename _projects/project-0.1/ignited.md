# IGNITED

## The 30-Day Content Experiment
*Project Zero Point One | Experiment Log*
*Start Date: 17 June 2026 | End Date: 17 July 2026*

---

## The Hypothesis

A five-format content repurposing system — built around GPS and SRT positioning, targeting one clear person with one clear problem and one clear solution, published consistently across 30 days — will produce measurable growth in followers, engagement, IGNITION guide downloads, and email list size beyond what unstructured daily posting produced in the preceding 22 days.

---

## The Experiment Parameters

| Variable | Value |
|---|---|
| **Platform** | TikTok only |
| **Duration** | 30 days — 17 June to 17 July 2026 |
| **Content system** | Five-format repurposing model |
| **Ideas bank** | 30 ideas × 5 formats = 150 pieces |
| **Posting cadence** | Up to 5 posts per day, minimum 1 |
| **Production method** | Batch sessions, scheduled via TikTok Studio |
| **Pillar structure** | 🗺️ Journey · 🎓 Lessons · 🔥 Mindset |
| **CTA system** | Format-specific CTAs per content strategy |
| **Lead magnet** | IGNITION guide — free download via Beacons |
| **Email capture** | Embedded in Beacons download flow |

**What stays constant for 30 days:**
- The strategy does not change mid-experiment
- The content pillars do not change
- The CTA system does not change
- Posting does not stop regardless of metrics

**What gets measured:**
- All TikTok metrics weekly
- IGNITION downloads weekly
- Email list size weekly
