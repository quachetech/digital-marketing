## Before You Begin

This is not a test. There are no right or wrong answers.

This is a mirror — a structured way of seeing clearly what the fifteen days actually produced. Most people finish a challenge and move straight to the next thing without stopping to name what changed. This audit makes the invisible visible.

Set aside 20–30 minutes. Find a quiet space. Answer honestly.

What you produce at the end of this audit is yours — a personal record of your own transformation that nobody else can write for you.
