**4. C16 — "Every way I turn it's no results." (26 likes)**

This is your audience talking directly to you. This comment alone is worth an entire content series. A funnel that works for Africans, built by an African, documented in real time — that's the entire IGNITION experiment reframed as a direct answer to this person.

**6. C11/C13 — "How do you differentiate?" / "This feels oversaturated." (11 likes)**
Saturation fear is one of the most common reasons beginners never start. Your answer — one person, one problem, one solution, plus the audacity to be seen — is exactly the antidote.
