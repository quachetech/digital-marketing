## M10 — Lower the Bar to Win

**Hook:**
Stop setting goals you cannot hit. Set goals you cannot miss.

**Body:**
I set a posting minimum of three videos per week.

I ended up posting every single day.

Not because I had unlimited motivation.

Because the floor was low enough to be winnable.

And winning feels good enough to keep going.

→ Momentum is built from wins — not from pressure
→ A standard you hit every day beats one you hit once a week
→ The minimum is the floor — not the ceiling
→ Once the floor is winnable, momentum carries you past it naturally

Most people set goals based on what they think they should be doing.

Then life happens. They miss a day. They conclude they are not consistent enough.

They are not inconsistent.

Their floor was just too high.

Lower the bar. Win every day. Raise it when the lower one is boring.

Consistency is a skill before it is a habit.
