## 1. Experiment Framing

This 90-day cycle is being run and tracked as a formal experiment with two distinct questions — not just a content calendar being executed. Both need to be answered honestly at Day 90, regardless of which way the data points.

**Primary Question — Is the strategy sound?**
Does the 90-day strategy documented in Modules 1–7 (carousel-locked Phase 1, list-building Phase 2, sales window Phase 3) produce results reasonably close to its own projected targets — 100 followers by Day 30, 250–300 subscribers by Day 75, 10 sales by Day 90?

**Meta Question — Are the skills behind it teachable?**
Separately from whether the numbers hit target: were the skills used to build this strategy — audience research, positioning (GPS/SRT), content architecture, funnel design, offer creation — applied in a way that is replicable and teachable to another beginner, the same way Account One's 15-day report became the foundation for the IGNITION guide?

> Both questions get answered at Day 90 with real evidence, not assumed in advance. A strategy that underperforms its targets can still validate the meta question if the reasoning behind it holds up and the mistakes are diagnosable — that outcome is still teachable material, just a different lesson than a clean win would be.

### Search Indexability Test — Original Hypothesis, Unconfirmed

Your own observation, kept explicitly separate from the confirmed research in the algorithm doc — this is a working hypothesis being tested with real data, not a claim.

**Working hypothesis:** A video needs a minimum live-time (roughly 24 hours, possibly up to 48) before Monolith finishes initial categorization and the video becomes eligible to appear in keyword search results — including results under the "Recently uploaded" filter, which itself appears to only surface videos that have been live long enough to be indexed at all.

**How it was observed:** Searched the exact terms used in Day 1's caption/hashtags — the post did not appear in Top results. The "Recently uploaded" filter (5/7-day window) surfaced other videos, which suggested a minimum live-duration floor for a video to be indexed at all, separate from whether it ranks well once indexed.

**What would confirm or break this:** if Day 1's post still doesn't appear in search after 24–48 hours live, that weakens the hypothesis (points to a keyword/ranking problem instead of an indexing-lag problem). If it does appear once past that window, that's a real supporting data point — still not proof with an n of 1, but a second and third post replicating the same pattern would make it worth stating with more confidence in the eventual guide.

## 6. Video / Footage Log

The eventual goal is a recap video — an honest account of what happened over the 90 days, in your voice, the same way the Journey pillar treats the TikTok content itself. The problem with building that at Day 90 from memory is that the specific, textured moments (the exact reaction to a bad week, the exact words used to explain a decision) get flattened into summary. This log exists so the raw material gets captured *while it's happening*, not reconstructed after.

There are two separate clocks here, and keeping them separate is the whole point: **when you record** (real time, as it happens) and **when you release** (delayed, on purpose, once Phase 2 opens the door to Talking Head content). Phase 1 is carousel-locked — nothing filmed here posts until at least Day 31.
