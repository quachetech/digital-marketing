# Project Zero Point One

## 15 Day Interim Content Plan

**Duration:** 15 days (post Project Zero)  
**Platform:** TikTok  
**Status:** Buffer period — maintaining momentum while preparing for Project One

---

## Goal

Build a consistent, recognizable content presence that attracts and retains a beginner digital marketing audience, while establishing core frameworks and voice ahead of the career guidance product launch.

---

## Objectives

|Objective|Target|Current Baseline|
|---|---|---|
|Consistency|Post at least once daily for 15 days|14 videos in 15 days|
|Audience Growth|Reach 25 followers by Day 15|12 followers|
|Engagement|15–20 genuine comments across all videos|~9 comments|

---

## Content Pillars

1. **Story/Mindset** — Personal experience, original frameworks, philosophy
2. **Education** — Digital marketing concepts explained simply for beginners
3. **Journey Update** — Transparent documentation of current learning and building

> Rule: Do not post the same pillar two days in a row.

---

## Call To Action Strategy

Every video ends with one of the following CTAs aligned to objectives:

- **For growth:** "Follow for more digital marketing tidbits from a beginner's perspective"
- **For engagement:** Ask one specific question e.g. "What's one myth you believed about digital marketing? Drop it in the comments"
- **For retention:** Tease the next video e.g. "Next video I'm breaking down [topic] — you don't want to miss it"

### Upcoming — Product Teaser Series

## Notes

- Scripts should have structure but not be read verbatim — use anchor points not full scripts
- Every video needs one clear CTA aligned to objectives
- Free flow delivery outperforms scripted delivery for personal/story content
- Structured delivery works better for education content
- Hooks are not a focus yet — that skill comes in Project One
- This plan is intentionally basic — advanced content strategy is a Project One skill
