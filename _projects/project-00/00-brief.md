## Project Overview

A fifteen-day live content creation challenge on TikTok, conducted on a brand new account built from zero. The challenge required posting consistently every day for fifteen days, following prompts and developing a creator identity in real time — with no prior TikTok presence, no existing audience, and no fixed content strategy.

---

## Context & Background

Project Zero is the first practicum in the Digital Marketing curriculum. It preceded all formal theoretical study and was initiated as part of the Digital Powerhouse programme — an online digital marketing programme with resale rights.

The challenge was not originally designed as part of a structured curriculum. It is being retrospectively integrated into the curriculum as **Phase One — The Blank Canvas** of the three-phase beginner content creator model developed from this experience.

*See: [00_foundations/philosophy.md](../../00-foundations/00-foundations-philosophy.md)* 

---

## Goals

- [ ] Post consistently on TikTok for fifteen consecutive days
- [ ] Build a creator identity on a brand new account from zero
- [ ] Develop basic content instincts — voice, format, angle, delivery
- [ ] Generate self-knowledge as a creator through doing, not planning
- [ ] Accumulate enough lived experience to inform a beginner content strategy

---

## Scope

**In scope:**
- Daily TikTok content creation and publishing
- Organic audience building from zero
- Creator identity development
- Platform familiarity (TikTok algorithm, engagement, discoverability)

**Out of scope:**
- Paid promotion or advertising
- Formal content strategy (by design — see philosophy)
- Revenue generation

---

## Target Audience

*As creator:*
English-speaking digital marketing learners and aspiring online entrepreneurs — people documenting their own learning journey and building in public.

*Challenge audience (for the packaged version):*
Complete beginners to content creation — people who have never posted consistently online and need a structured entry point before strategy is introduced.
