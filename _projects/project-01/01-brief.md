## Project Overview

A 60–90 day live practicum in which a career guidance digital product suite — comprising a self-guided workbook and a facilitator guide — is designed, built, marketed, and sold using foundational digital marketing skills acquired concurrently through theoretical study.

The product is the vehicle. The project is the education.

---

## Context & Background

Project One is the second practicum in the Digital Marketing curriculum and the first revenue-generating live project. It follows Project Zero (the Fifteen-Day Content Challenge) and runs concurrently with theoretical study across Modules 01–05.

The career guidance product suite was originally conceived as a consulting service, pivoted to digital products due to capacity constraints. Foundational materials — discovery question sets, Career Life Plan template, session flow guides, intake form — already exist and require expansion rather than rebuilding.

The Digital Powerhouse programme is a natural embedded recommendation within both products in the digital careers section.

## Goals

**Product**
- [ ] Complete and package the Self-Guided Workbook for high school leavers
- [ ] Complete and package the Facilitator Guide for educators, mentors, and coaches
- [ ] Write product copy — clear, benefit-led descriptions for both products

**Marketing**
- [ ] Define 2–3 content pillars anchoring all marketing content
- [ ] Build a basic content strategy and content calendar
- [ ] Publish content consistently across at least one platform
- [ ] Begin building an audience around the product

**Sales Infrastructure**
- [ ] Build a functioning landing page for the product suite
- [ ] Integrate a working payment platform
- [ ] Set up email capture from day one of marketing
- [ ] Build a simple automated delivery system
- [ ] Design a basic sales funnel: awareness → interest → decision → purchase

**Learning**
- [ ] Document each skill cluster engaged with corresponding reflection notes
- [ ] Pass the articulation test at project close

---

## Scope

**In scope:**
- Career guidance digital product suite (workbook + facilitator guide)
- Organic content marketing on chosen platform(s)
- Basic sales infrastructure (landing page, payment, email, delivery)
- Skill documentation and learning debrief

**Out of scope:**
- Paid advertising (budget not available at this stage)
- Advanced automation or CRM systems
- Revenue targets — this is a learning vehicle, not a business plan

---

## Target Audience

**Self-Guided Workbook:**
High school leavers navigating their next steps independently — young people who need structured guidance but do not have access to a counsellor, mentor, or structured programme.

**Facilitator Guide:**
Educators, mentors, coaches, and youth workers running career guidance sessions — people who need a done-for-you system they can deploy without extensive preparation.

## Constraints & Considerations

## The Theory-Practice Link

This project runs concurrently with the theoretical curriculum. For each skill cluster engaged, the corresponding module must be studied at foundational level before or during its application. Knowledge from the classroom feeds immediately into project decisions. Project experience feeds immediately back into classroom understanding.

Neither runs ahead of the other by more than one week.
