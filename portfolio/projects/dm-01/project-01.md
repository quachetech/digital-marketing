## The One-Line Summary

Designing, building, marketing, and selling a career guidance digital product suite — a self-guided workbook and a facilitator guide — using foundational digital marketing skills acquired concurrently through independent study.

---

## The Challenge

Take an existing body of career guidance material and turn it into a complete, market-ready digital product suite. Then build the marketing infrastructure and sales system to bring it to a real audience — from scratch, on a limited budget, while studying the theory needed to do it at the same time.

The product is the vehicle. The project is the education.

---

## The Products

**1. Self-Guided Workbook**
For high school leavers navigating their next steps independently. A structured, accessible resource for young people who need guidance but don't have access to a counsellor, mentor, or formal programme.

**2. Facilitator Guide**
For educators, mentors, coaches, and youth workers. A done-for-you system for running career guidance sessions — structured, practical, and ready to deploy without extensive preparation.

## Success Criteria

At project close, the following will be true:

- Both products are complete, packaged, and available for purchase
- A content strategy has been executed consistently and is documented
- A functioning landing page, payment system, email capture, and delivery process are live
- At minimum one sale has been made
- Every skill engaged is documented with honest reflection notes
- The articulation test is passed — every component of the system can be explained clearly in own words
