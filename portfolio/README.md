## What This Is

This portfolio is the public-facing layer of a private learning journey. It does not showcase perfection — it showcases progression. Every project here was built at the edge of current knowledge, with real stakes, in real time.

These are not simulations. They are not hypothetical case studies. They are live projects — built, launched, and documented by a learner who was also the entrepreneur, the marketer, the writer, and the strategist.

---

## About the Work

All projects in this portfolio are live practicums — part of an independent digital marketing curriculum being developed and studied concurrently. Each project served two purposes simultaneously:

1. **A learning vehicle** — building and demonstrating a specific cluster of digital marketing skills
2. **A real product or campaign** — designed for a real audience, with a real outcome

The work is documented in full, including what worked, what didn't, what was learned, and what Version 2 will look like.

## A Note on Documentation

Every project in this portfolio includes a full process log and outcomes document. The process log is written *during* the work — messy, honest, real-time. The outcomes document is written *after* — structured, reflective, analytical.

Both matter. The process log is where the learning lives. The outcomes document is where it crystallises.
